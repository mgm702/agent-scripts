# Marketing Plan

**Marketing is sales at scale — but it is not advertising.** Make fans, not headlines. Spend time before money: blogs, social, and email are free. Only spend on ads once you know exactly who you're reaching.

## When to Use

- The user has product-market fit (~100 paying, repeat customers) and wants to scale
- They need a content strategy that doesn't depend on an ad budget
- They've sold one-on-one and want to reach strangers at scale

**Prerequisites:** a community you belong to, a product people pay for, ~100 customers, and experience selling one-on-one (sales informs marketing).

## The Funnel

Every customer goes through all five steps — you can't skip any:
**Engage → Follow → Research → Consider → Buy**

## Three Levels of Content

Progress through these; each reaches a wider audience:
1. **Educate** — share what you've learned building the business. 100 customers = 100 things worth sharing. Consistency over polish.
2. **Inspire** — share the journey, struggles, and wins. Be vulnerable and authentic.
3. **Entertain** — hardest but most far-reaching. Entertainment is king on every platform.

## Channels

- **Social** — keep a personal and a business account. Be authentic, build in public, give don't ask. Pick *one* platform that fits your business rather than juggling all of them. Trust the feedback loop.
- **Email = owned land** — social is rented (algorithms change, accounts get shut down). Build an email list immediately; offer something valuable (guide, checklist) in exchange. Each subscriber is worth far more than a follower.
- **Content calendar** — pick 1–2 platforms + email, post on a sustainable schedule. Quality over quantity.

## Spend Money Last

- Most "organic" growth you see is actually paid. Don't be fooled.
- Only spend on ads after organic traction and a clear customer profile; then use lookalike audiences.
- Spend on existing customers (rewards, loyalty) before acquisition.
- Never pay more to acquire a customer than you make from one.

## Output

Help the user produce:
1. Their primary content platform and posting schedule
2. 5 content ideas at each level (educate / inspire / entertain)
3. An email list strategy (what to offer, how to collect)
4. A "build in public" plan — what to share from their journey
5. When (if ever) to consider paid ads
